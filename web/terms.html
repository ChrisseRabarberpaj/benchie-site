What Benchie is

A map of public benches with ratings and check-ins from people like you. It is for sitting, not for anything you would need a lawyer for.

You can report or block anyone from their profile in the app. A person reads every report, and three reports about someone reset their display name.

Being at the bench

Check-ins require you to be at the bench. Faking location, scripting check-ins or ratings, or running more than one account to climb a leaderboard gets the account removed. The benches would not respect it either.

The map

Bench positions come from OpenStreetMap under the Open Database License and are offered as they are. A bench may have moved, been removed, or be on private ground. Use your eyes. Benches you add to Benchie may be contributed back to OpenStreetMap under the ODbL. The bench positions Benchie uses, the added ones included, are available under the same licence: write to hello@benchie.world and you get them as a file.

Your safety

Presence and calls show people where you are sitting, for a limited time, to the audience you choose. Only turn them on if you are comfortable with that. Meet people in public places. Benchie does not vet anyone.
