Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026. Applies to the Benchie app for iOS and Android and to benchie.world.

Benchie is made by Talecraft, a sole proprietorship registered in Finland, run by Christoffer Linder (the "we" below). We are the data controller. You can reach us at hello@benchie.world.

The short version

Benchie stores what you do with benches (check-ins, ratings, badges, friends) under an account id. It checks your location once, at the moment you check in, to confirm you are at the bench, and does not store your position or track you. It does not sell data. Unless you are a Supporter, it shows small ads from Google AdMob, which in Europe only asks to personalise them after you say yes. If you rate a hundred benches and ask for the tester envelope, we use the address you give us once, to post it, and then delete it.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. Benchie itself does not use advertising identifiers; Google's ad service does, as far as your consent choice allows (see Ads below). We do not run analytics that profile you. We do not read your contacts. We do not record your location except at the moment of a check-in, and then only to answer "is this person at the bench".

Where the data lives

Our database and authentication are run by Supabase, Inc. on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap, which sees your IP address and the map areas you look at, as any map does. Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies; we receive an id and, if you allow it, your email address. Photos of benches you add are sent once to Anthropic, an AI provider, to check that they show a bench; only the photo is sent, nothing about you. Notifications are delivered through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging, and on iPhone through Apple's push service. They receive your phone's notification token and the text of each notification, nothing else.

Who can see what

Always public: your display name and worn badge, your check-in counts and badges, your position on leaderboards, and the scores and notes on your ratings (bench scores are the product). The rest is yours to set, in the app under Profile, Who sees what, each to everyone, friends or nobody: whether people see you sitting on a bench right now (friends only, until you change it), whether your name is on your ratings and photos (otherwise they show as "A sitter"), whether what you do appears in other people's activity feed (friends only, until you change it), and whether your five best benches show on your profile. Changes apply straight away.

Two rules sit behind those settings. Friends see what you do as it happens; everyone else sees the day, never the time, and the Nearby feed shows them only that a bench got a rating, from the next day, unless you chose to be named there. A friend you add sees what you do from that moment on, not before, and unfriending or blocking someone removes their view at once. Only you: your email address. Only us: your postal address while the envelope is being sent, and technical logs.

Ads and subscriptions

Ads. If you are not a Supporter, Benchie shows a small banner at the end of a bench card and in the "Best here" list, never on the map. The ads come from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd). To show and count them, Google receives your phone's advertising identifier, IP address, a rough location worked out from it, the device type and how you interact with the ad. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks first, using Google's consent message, whether ads may be personalised; you can change your answer at any time in Profile > Ad privacy choices. Your Benchie data (ratings, check-ins, where you sit, friends) is never given to Google or used to pick ads. Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Benchie Supporter. The subscription is sold and charged by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to know which Benchie account has an active subscription: it receives your Benchie account id and the store receipt, nothing else. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU standard contractual clauses. You cancel in your Google Play or App Store account; Profile > Benchie Supporter has the link.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. The quickest route: in the app, Profile > Delete account removes everything attributed to you, including your ratings and check-ins, immediately. Without the app: see benchie.world/delete.html. For anything else, email hello@benchie.world and we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

Benchie is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you on its next launch before anything new is collected.
