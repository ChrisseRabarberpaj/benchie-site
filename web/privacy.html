Benchie

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026. Applies to the Benchie app for iOS and Android and to benchie.world.

Benchie is made by Talecraft, a sole proprietorship registered in Finland, run by Christoffer Linder (the "we" below). We are the data controller. You can reach us at hello@benchie.world.

The short version

Benchie stores what you do with benches (check-ins, ratings, badges, friends) under an account id. It checks your location once, at the moment you check in, to confirm you are at the bench, and does not store your position or track you. It does not sell data. If you rate a hundred benches and ask for the tester envelope, we use the address you give us once, to post it, and then delete it.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising identifiers to track you across apps. We do not run analytics that profile you. We do not read your contacts. We do not record your location except at the moment of a check-in, and then only to answer "is this person at the bench".

Where the data lives

Our database and authentication are run by Supabase, Inc. on servers in the European Union (Ireland). Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which sees your IP address and the map areas you look at, as any map does. Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies; we receive an id and, if you allow it, your email address. Photos of benches you add are sent once to Anthropic, an AI provider, to check that they show a bench; only the photo is sent, nothing about you.

Who can see what

Public: your display name, worn badge, ratings and the notes and photos on them, your check-in counts and badges, and your position on leaderboards (you can hide yourself from boards in the app; you still count, just not by name). Friends, or everyone if you choose: that you are on a specific bench right now. Only you: your email address. Only us: your postal address while the envelope is being sent, and technical logs.

Ads and subscriptions

A future version may show ads to non-subscribers and offer a Benchie Supporter subscription. If and when it does, this policy will be updated first, the ad provider will be named here, and ads will not use your Benchie data for targeting. Subscriptions are handled by Apple and Google; we never see your payment details.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. The quickest route: in the app, Profile > Delete account removes everything attributed to you, including your ratings and check-ins, immediately. For anything else, email hello@benchie.world and we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

Benchie is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you on its next launch before anything new is collected.
