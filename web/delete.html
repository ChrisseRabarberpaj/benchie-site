Delete your account

Benchie, by Talecraft (Christoffer Linder), Helsinki, Finland. Last updated 10 October 2026.

In the app (fastest)

Open Benchie, go to Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. It happens at once.

Without the app

Email hello@benchie.world with the subject "Delete my Benchie account". So we delete the right one, include:

  • if you kept your account with email, Apple or Google: write from that email address, or tell us which one it was;
  • if you never kept it (an anonymous account): your display name in Benchie and, if you remember, your friend code or a bench you rated.

We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything attributed to you: your account, display name, check-ins, ratings and their notes and photos, badges, friends, nods, meetups you hosted and your answers to others, blocks, notifications and the reports you filed. Benches you added stay on the map without your name, because other people sit on them too. Technical logs are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted. If you asked for the tester envelope, your postal address was already deleted once it was posted.
